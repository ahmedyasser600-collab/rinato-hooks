## The method, in our own words

The traditional five-act arc (conflict → rising action → climax → falling action → resolution)
starts low and builds slowly. On short-form video the viewer is gone before the rising action
begins.

The Modern Story Arc changes the shape of the tension curve (0–100):

1. **Start at 70.** The first ~5 s should feel like being dropped into a story already in motion.
   No setup, no backstory, no "let me explain".
2. **Spike to a first peak at 90 fast.** A contrast moment, a contrarian take or a shocking stat,
   within about 5 s.
3. **Release, then peak again.** In short-form, build to a new peak every 10–15 s: peak, release,
   peak, release.
4. **No single climax in the middle.** Stack mini-peaks and rehook throughout.

How this skill applies it:
- `SKILL.md` and `references/hook-types.md`: the hook is the 70 start and, ideally, the first peak.
- `references/arc.md`: the peak/release map with the beat types.
- `scripts/tension_curve.py`: maps a script's beats onto the curve, and flags a slow open, a late
  first peak, or a flat stretch longer than 15 s.
