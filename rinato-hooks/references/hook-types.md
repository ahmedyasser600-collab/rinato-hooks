# Hook types

Each type has a shape (a template with blanks), why it stops the scroll, the trap that makes it
weak, and examples for small-business niches. Examples are Italian with an English gloss; the
shapes work in any language.

`[N]` is a blank for a real fact from the facts sheet. Never fill it with a guess. Numbers that
are true by design (1 QR code, 0 apps, 4 steps, a clock time in a scene) are always safe.

These are starting points, not finished copy. Say every line out loud before you keep it.

---

## 1. Direct question (`question`)

**Shape:** `[concrete question about the viewer's own day]?` + a frame that shows the answer is coming.
**Why it works:** the viewer answers in their head, and now it's about them.
**Trap:** the vague yes/no question nobody needs answered ("Vuoi più clienti?").

| Niche | Example |
| --- | --- |
| Websites | "Il tuo sito si apre in quanti secondi sul telefono?" (How many seconds does your site take to open on a phone?) |
| Café loyalty | "Dove sono finite le tessere timbro che hai stampato?" (Where did the stamp cards you printed end up?) |
| Student rentals | "Quante stanze hai visto solo in foto prima di firmare?" (How many rooms did you only see in photos before signing?) |
| Renovation | "Sai quanto costa davvero rifare un bagno?" (Do you know what a bathroom redo really costs?) Only if the video shows a real figure. |
| Gym | "When did you last test your 1-rep max?" |
| Home repair | "Quanti tecnici hai chiamato prima che uno rispondesse?" (How many technicians did you call before one picked up?) |

## 2. "Don't do X" warning (`dont`)

**Shape:** `Non [common action] prima di [guardare questo / sapere X].`
**Why it works:** loss aversion. Nobody wants to be the one making the mistake.
**Trap:** warning against something the viewer isn't about to do.

| Niche | Example |
| --- | --- |
| Branding | "Non rifare il logo prima di guardare questo." (Don't redo your logo before you see this.) |
| Café loyalty | "Non ristampare le tessere timbro." (Don't reprint your stamp cards.) |
| Student rentals | "Non mandare la caparra prima di questa chiamata." (Don't send the deposit before this call.) |
| Renovation | "Non buttare giù quel muro prima di saperlo." (Don't knock down that wall before you know this.) |
| Home repair | "Non pagare l'uscita prima di vedere il preventivo." (Don't pay the call-out fee before you see the quote.) |

## 3. "If you're not doing X, you're doing it wrong" (`wrong`)

**Shape:** `Se [il tuo X] non [fa Y], stai sbagliando.`
**Why it works:** a direct, slightly uncomfortable challenge to a habit.
**Trap:** sounding preachy. Keep it about a habit, never the person, and make Y concrete.

| Niche | Example |
| --- | --- |
| Websites | "Se il tuo sito non prende prenotazioni di notte, stai perdendo clienti." (If your site doesn't take bookings at night, you're losing customers.) |
| Café loyalty | "Se la tua tessera fedeltà è di carta, la stai regalando al cestino." (If your loyalty card is paper, you're giving it to the bin.) |
| Renovation | "Se il preventivo non ha le voci separate, non firmarlo." (If the quote doesn't itemise, don't sign it.) |
| Gym | "If you only train what you're good at, you're training wrong." |

## 4. Contrarian claim (`contrarian`)

**Shape:** `[Widely held belief] è sbagliato.` or `Smetti di [consiglio comune].`
**Why it works:** contrast. The viewer stays to see if you can back it up.
**Trap:** contrarian for its own sake. The video must prove it. Never "Non è X, è Y" (an AI tell).

| Niche | Example |
| --- | --- |
| Websites | "Il tuo locale non ha bisogno di un'app." (Your shop doesn't need an app.) |
| Student rentals | "La stanza più economica è quasi sempre quella che costa di più." (The cheapest room is almost always the one that costs most.) |
| Gym | "Rest days are where you get stronger." |
| Home repair | "Il tecnico più vicino non è il più veloce." (The nearest technician isn't the fastest.) |

## 5. Specific number (`number`)

**Shape:** `[Number] [concrete unit] + [what it means for you].` The number is big, on screen, on frame 0.
**Why it works:** a precise number reads as true (02:14 beats "late at night").
**Trap:** invented stats. Use numbers true by design, real project numbers, or a cited source.

| Niche | Example |
| --- | --- |
| Websites | "02:14. Il tuo negozio è chiuso. Il tuo sito no." (02:14. Your shop is closed. Your site isn't.) |
| Café loyalty | "0 app da scaricare. 1 QR sul bancone." (0 apps to download. 1 QR on the counter.) |
| Café loyalty | "Regala 2 timbri: quasi il doppio delle tessere arriva al premio." (Nunes & Drèze 2006: 34% vs 19%.) |
| Websites | "Da 1 a 3 secondi di caricamento: +32% di probabilità che chiudano." (Think with Google: bounce probability.) |

## 6. Pattern interrupt (`interrupt`)

**Shape:** frame 0 shows something that should not be happening, and the line names it in under 1 s.
**Why it works:** the brain flags the unexpected before it decides to scroll.
**Trap:** a weird image with no link to the topic. The interrupt must *be* the pain point.

| Niche | Example |
| --- | --- |
| Websites | A loading bar crawls, a red X slams it: "Tre secondi. Il cliente è già andato." |
| Café loyalty | A paper stamp card goes into a shredder: "Questa era la tua tessera fedeltà." |
| Student rentals | The bright listing photo drains to the real dark room: "La foto dell'annuncio. La stanza vera." |
| Home repair | A call counter climbs to 9, none answered: "Nove chiamate per un rubinetto." |

## 7–9. Retention types (`story`, `emotional`, `confession`)

- **Story / POV:** open mid-scene, at 70 on the curve. "Sabato, ore 13. Il telefono del negozio
  non smette di vibrare."
- **Emotional:** name the viewer's struggle precisely. "Questo è per chi chiude alle 20 e
  risponde ai messaggi fino alle 23."
- **Confession:** only the user's own true story. "Zero visualizzazioni. Ora i nostri video li
  boccia un'AI."

---

## Matching type to video

- A product explainer → `dont`, `wrong` or `number`: they promise a fix.
- A story video → `number`, `interrupt` or `story`: they drop you into a moment.
- An opinion video → `contrarian`, with proof in the body.
- An event → `question` or `number` with a date.

The 3 options should be 3 different types, so the choice is a real choice.
